Use the term of the bounded context you are talking about, and never mix them in one sentence:

- Scheduling: a **booking** (start, end, staff member).
- Customers: a **visit**.
- Billing: a **charge** (amount and payment status).
- Notifications: a **reminder**.

When an answer crosses contexts, name the context first ("In Billing, ...") and then use that context's word. Keep answers short and in English.
